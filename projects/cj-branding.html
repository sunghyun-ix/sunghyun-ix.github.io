Professional Project · 2012–2015

CJ Group Entertainment Ecosystem Branding Strategy

Role: Sr. Brand Marketing Analyst Period: Jan 2012 – Sep 2015 Organization: CJ Corporation, Seoul, Republic of Korea

Overview

Developed comprehensive branding strategies for CJ Group's extensive entertainment business portfolio, including 15 television channels, ten music labels, film investment & distribution, and multiplex movie theaters.

Created and implemented the strategic branding framework for KCON, the world's largest Hallyu (Korean Wave) Convention & Show, successfully expanding the event to eight countries worldwide.

This work involved deep analysis of cross-cultural media consumption patterns, audience behavior segmentation, and strategic positioning of Korean entertainment content in global markets. The project significantly contributed to the international expansion of the Korean entertainment industry.
