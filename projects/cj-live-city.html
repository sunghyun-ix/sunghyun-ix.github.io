Professional Project · 2015–2020

CJ LIVE CITY Theme Park Development

Role: Project Manager & CX Strategic Designer Period: Oct 2015 – Jul 2020 Organization: CJ LIVE CITY, Seoul, Republic of Korea

Overview

Led a US$1.5 billion project to develop an innovative offline entertainment platform spanning 330,578m² of land. The project involved creating trans-platform connected experiences that seamlessly integrated online and offline entertainment through ride attractions and interactive technologies.

Designed and managed budget allocation for innovative walk-through attractions that combined virtual reality with physical activity, creating immersive visitor experiences using advanced gamification methods.

This project represented a groundbreaking approach to entertainment design, blending digital storytelling with physical space to create unprecedented user experiences.
