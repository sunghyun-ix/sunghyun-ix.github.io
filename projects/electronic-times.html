Professional Project · 2007–2011

Digital Media Innovation Research

Role: News Reporter & Industry Analyst Period: Oct 2007 – Jan 2011 Organization: The Electronic Times, Seoul, Republic of Korea

Overview

Conducted extensive investigative journalism and industry analysis at Korea's leading technology and innovation-focused daily newspaper. Produced over 3,500 short articles and 135 feature pieces, specializing in emerging technology trends and their societal impacts.

Notable investigative projects included “Digital Aging” (2010), examining the psychological and social implications of rapid technological change on different demographic groups, and “Election-Social media viral effect” (2009), analyzing the impact of social media on political communication and voter behavior.

This work provided foundational insights into the intersection of technology, media, and human behavior that continues to inform current research approaches.
